Download WebcamLR

You need two pieces: the app on the phone (iPhone or Android), which is the camera and the remote, and the app on the computer (Mac or Windows), which receives the video. Every combination works.

Not released yet. The iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows apps are in testing on real devices: the buttons turn on as soon as each version is ready. Meanwhile you can build it from source.

For those who want to try it now

Build it from source

The code is on GitHub. For iPhone and Mac you need a Mac with Xcode and an Apple ID; with a free Apple ID everything works except the Mac’s virtual webcam. For Windows and Android the step-by-step instructions are in Documenti/Installazione e prova - tutte le piattaforme.md (in Italian): for Windows you need the .NET 8 SDK and a double-click on Strumenti/Costruisci e avvia.bat, for Android, Android Studio opened on the Android folder.

iPhone and Mac

  1. In Terminal, download the code and switch to the latest branch, the one in testing:git clone https://github.com/LR-apps609/WebcamLR.git cd WebcamLR git checkout round-2
  2. Double-click Strumenti/Costruisci e installa.command. The first time it asks for your Apple Team ID and whether you have a paid developer account, and installs XcodeGen with Homebrew.
  3. The script puts WebcamLR in Applications and opens the iPhone project in Xcode: choose your iPhone at the top and press ▶.
  4. For the phone microphone: double-click Strumenti/Installa microfono.command (it asks for your Mac password).

After downloading, on the Mac

  1. Open the .dmg file and drag WebcamLR to Applications; for the microphone, also open “WebcamLR Microfono.pkg”.
  2. Open WebcamLR on the Mac and on the phone: the phone finds the Mac by itself, or you scan the QR code or plug in the cable.
  3. For the virtual webcam, click Turn on virtual webcam in the “Outputs” column and approve it in System Settings (on macOS 15 and later: General › Login Items & Extensions › Camera Extensions; on macOS 14: Privacy & Security › Allow).
  4. Quit and reopen your call apps, then choose “WebcamLR” as the camera.

Something not working? The help page has the fixes and the same FAQ as the app.
