The best webcam you own is already in your pocket.

WebcamLR turns the camera of your iPhone, or your Android phone, into a webcam for your Mac or PC, over USB cable, Wi-Fi or hotspot. It connects by itself, tells you what to do when something’s wrong, and you control it from the phone with a tap.

How it works

Three steps, one minute.

  1. 01

    Install WebcamLR on the computer

    A Mac with macOS 14 or later, or a PC with 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

  2. 02

    Open the app on the phone

    An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or a phone with Android 8 or later. On the same network the computer shows up by itself; or scan the computer’s QR code, or plug in the cable.

  3. 03

    The video arrives on the computer

    In the WebcamLR window, with the camera controls; with the virtual webcam, in your call apps too. When an app is using it, the phone and the computer show “Live”.

Connections

Four ways, chosen by itself.

WebcamLR uses the best way it finds and always shows it to you, on the phone and on the computer, with the quality in words: “Excellent”, “Good” or “Weak”.

USB cable

The most stable

Plug in the cable and the video starts by itself while the phone charges. If you’re already on Wi-Fi it moves to the cable without stopping; unplug it and it goes back to Wi-Fi.

On the Mac it works with the version downloaded from this site: the Mac App Store doesn’t allow it. With Android you use “USB tethering”.

Wi-Fi

Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network: the computer shows up by itself, or with the QR code. When you open the app it reconnects by itself to the last computer you used.

Hotel, office and school networks often keep devices apart: there you need the cable or the hotspot.

Hotspot

Turn on the phone’s hotspot and connect the computer to it: a network just for the two of them, anywhere.

The video stays between the phone and the computer: it doesn’t go over the internet.

Wi-Fi Direct

Mac only

iPhone and Mac find each other even without a router, with the same technology as AirDrop. Turn it on in WebcamLR’s Settings on the Mac.

Latency is less stable than on your home network: keep it off when you don’t need it.

Secure pairing

Your video goes only to your computer.

No account, no server in between: the video travels inside your network or over the cable, encrypted. Read the privacy policy

  • The computer’s QR code contains its security code: by scanning it, the phone knows it’s talking to the right computer.
  • Without the QR code, the first time the computer asks “Allow…?” with 4 digits, the same ones you see on the phone.
  • Video, audio and controls encrypted (AES-256), with new keys for every connection.
  • From the second time on, the trusted phone connects without asking; you can remove it from the computer whenever you like.

Questions

The short answers.

Do I need a cable?

No: the phone and the computer just need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. The USB cable is the most stable choice and charges the phone at the same time; if you plug it in during a call, the video moves to the cable without stopping.

Does it work with Zoom, Meet, Teams and FaceTime?

Yes, through the virtual webcam: on Windows 10 and 11 it’s part of the app in testing; on the Mac it’s already written and ships with the version signed with the Apple developer account. Until then, on the Mac the video shows in the WebcamLR window.

Does the video go over the internet?

No. The video goes from the phone to the computer only over your network or the cable, encrypted with new keys for every connection. It doesn’t pass through our servers and isn’t saved anywhere. Privacy

Where do I choose profiles?

On the computer, in the WebcamLR window, with WebcamLR Pro: the phone is mostly a remote, with Mirror, Blur background, Microphone, Flashlight, Pause and Settings.

Which devices do I need?

A phone, an iPhone with iOS 17 or later or Android 8 or later, and a computer, a Mac with macOS 14 or later or a PC with 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Every combination works. Linux is under consideration.

How much does it cost?

Nothing, up to 720p at 30 fps and with no watermark. WebcamLR Pro costs €14.99 once (€9.99 at launch); you buy it on the phone and it unlocks the computer too. No subscription, no account.
