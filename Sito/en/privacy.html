Privacy · updated 30 October 2026 · draft

Your video stays yours.

In short

  • Video and audio go from the phone straight to the computer, inside your network or over the USB cable. They don’t go over the internet or through our servers.
  • Video, audio and controls travel encrypted (AES-256), with new keys for every connection: not even someone on the same network can see them.
  • A new phone can connect only with the QR code shown by your computer, or after you confirm it yourself with a 4-digit code.
  • We don’t record, keep or look at anything you film.
  • No account is needed, not even for WebcamLR Pro. The apps contain no ads and send us no data. On Android, the Google library that computes the effects (MediaPipe) may send anonymous usage statistics to Google: we say so until it’s turned off.

Who is responsible

The controller’s name, address and email will be published here before the apps are released.

Photos and recordings made from the computer are saved only on the computer, in its pictures and videos folders, under WebcamLR.

On their own, the Mac and Windows apps use the internet for one thing only: they read the public file versione.json from this site at launch and every 6 hours, to show “Update available”: they send no data, but as with any visit, GitHub Pages sees the IP address. If .NET 8 is missing on the PC, the Windows installer downloads it from the internet, from Microsoft’s servers. “Report a bug” prepares a message with your description and, if you want, technical data and the connection log (version, device, latency): the log may contain device names, for example “Connected to …’s MacBook”. You see it before sending and can leave it out; you send it yourself, with the email or sharing method you choose.

Purchases

WebcamLR Pro is bought on the phone: on the iPhone the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store, on Android by Google through Google Play. We don’t receive your card details or your account; only the purchase code stays on the phone. The Mac and Windows apps sell nothing: they only ask the connected phone whether it has Pro.

This site

The site uses no cookies and no analytics, and the fonts are hosted here, with no outside services. It only remembers, in your browser, whether you chose a language or closed the “Leggi questa pagina in italiano” strip, and whether you stopped the home page animations: these choices never leave your device. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security: see their privacy statement.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its use. Since the apps send us no personal data, we usually have nothing to show you; the address to write to will be given above. You can also contact the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
